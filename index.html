Copywriting

Every line has a job.

Most small business ads and websites describe the business. Mine are written to move a customer from “just looking” to “ringing now”.

Lines I'd cross out

You'll find these on most trade websites. Here's what I'd write instead. These are examples, not taken from any one business.

The opening line

We are a family-run business with over 20 years of experience.

Every competitor says this. Use a fact only you have.

Fitting kitchens around Naas since 2004, and we still answer our own phone.

The price

Quality workmanship at competitive prices.

Nobody believes “competitive”. Tell them how pricing works.

You get a fixed price in writing before we start. It doesn't change when we open up the wall.

The last line

Contact us today for more information.

Say exactly what happens next.

Send a photo of the problem on WhatsApp and get a price back the same day.

The Roofit page, line by line

The top of a page I wrote for a roofer in Carlisle, and why each part is there.

  1. 1

    Lead with the outcome

    A flat roof that stays dry for 30 years.

    People don't want “GRP roofing”. They want to stop worrying about the roof. So that comes first.

  2. 2

    Name the pain they already feel

    Not one you patch every winter.

    The reader sees their own situation and thinks, “that's me”. That's when they keep reading.

  3. 3

    Answer “can I trust them?” early

    Trading since 1989 · Fully insured · 24hr call-out

    Three facts that take away the risk before the customer even thinks to ask.

  4. 4

    One clear, easy next step

    Book a free roof survey

    Free, specific and low commitment. “Contact us” tells nobody what happens next.

Straight up

What you won't get from me.

  • Likes reported as results

    Reach and likes don't pay for anything. I report on calls, messages and enquiries.

  • Promises of “100 leads a month”

    Nobody can promise that before an ad has run. I'll tell you what I expect, then show you what actually happened.

  • Your ad budget in my account

    Ad spend is paid straight to Meta or Google from your own account. You see every cent, and you can walk away whenever you like.

  • Fake reviews or made-up numbers

    If you don't have reviews yet, your ads won't pretend you do. Real ones go in as they arrive.

  • Stock photos passed off as your work

    Until you have your own photos, I use licensed stock or simple graphics, never labelled as your jobs.

  • Going live half-finished

    Nothing goes public until every detail is confirmed and every form, button and tracking tag is tested.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Do you run the ads, or just write them?

Both. I write the ads, set up who sees them, and keep an eye on the results. You decide the budget, and the ad spend is paid straight to Meta or Google from your own account, so you always see exactly where your money goes.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. Writing and setting up your ads starts from [YOUR STARTING PRICE], and running them is [YOUR MONTHLY PRICE] a month, plus whatever you choose to spend on the ads themselves. Either way, you get a fixed price in writing before any work starts.

How much should I spend on ads?

Enough for a fair test, and no more until something's working. We agree a small budget first, find the ad that brings in enquiries, then decide together whether to spend more.

Do I need a new website before I run ads?

Not always. If your site loads quickly and makes it easy to call or message you, the ads can send people straight there. If it doesn't, a single landing page built for the ad is usually quicker and cheaper than a whole new site.

How quickly will I see results?

Ads and a better Google Business Profile can bring enquiries within weeks. Ranking higher on Google for free usually takes months. I'll always tell you which is which, rather than promise something I can't control.

I don't have good photos or any reviews. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Several of my projects started without one or the other. I use licensed stock photos or simple graphics until you have your own, and leave reviews out until real ones come in.

Do you work outside Dublin?

Yes. I've worked on projects for businesses across Ireland, Scotland and England. Everything can be done over WhatsApp, phone, email or a video call.
