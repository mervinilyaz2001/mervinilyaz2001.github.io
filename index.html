    Until you have your own photos, I use credited stock or simple illustrations, never labelled as your jobs.

    Your site is hand-coded, so it loads fast and doesn't depend on a monthly builder subscription.

    Your site stays hidden from Google until every detail is confirmed and every form is tested.
